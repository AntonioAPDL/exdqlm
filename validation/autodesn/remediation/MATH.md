# Executed target and transition specification

This records the implemented conditional model, not a claim of convergence or predictive improvement. The feature bank, centering/scaling and candidate admission are fixed from training. As in the approved plan, response-adaptive preprocessing does not define an unconditional generative prior over raw training sequences; fixed-design and independently calibrated recursive SBC have distinct scopes.

For architecture `m` and period index `p`, the standardized design is `[1,L,H_m,S_p]`. Priors factor as `p(m)p(p)` with the exact original positive reservoir masses and explicit normalized period masses. There is no heuristic removal of architectures or periods. A fixed auxiliary space has distinct slots for lag, reservoir and calendar families; every candidate uses distinct active coordinates. Inactive normalized scale priors integrate to one.

The intercept is N(0,v0), default v0=10. All coefficient priors are independent of noise variance. In the flat normalized RHS, every slope has variance `v_j=(1/(tau^2 lambda_j^2)+1/c2)^(-1)`, `tau=tau0*u`, u and each lambda standard half-Cauchy, and c2~IG(df/2,df*slab_scale^2/2). In the grouped model lag/reservoir groups have independent u_g, shared c2, and calendar harmonics have independent proper Gaussian variances `calendar_scale^2/(1+(k/4)^4)`. Fixed local/global/slab controls retain their explicit deterministic meaning. Ridge assigns precision lambda to all non-Gaussian-calendar slopes with a proper Gamma(shape,rate) hyperprior when learned. The intercept never enters the ridge scale update.

For diagonal observation covariance D and diagonal proper coefficient covariance V, the exact Gaussian conditional is N(A^-1 r,A^-1), A=V^-1+X'D^-1X, r=X'D^-1y. Integrated log likelihood is `-n/2 log(2pi)-1/2(log|D|+log|V|+log|A|+y'D^-1y-r'A^-1r)`. Production uses prior-whitened Cholesky and a positive residual-plus-prior quadratic, not a cancellation-prone subtraction or explicit inverse. Weighted C++ uses transformed X/y and subtracts one half the observation log determinant. Independently constructed dense observation-space covariances validate the result.

Architecture/period MH includes the integrated log likelihood difference, both normalized prior-mass differences and both proposal-mass ratios. Joint moves additionally include transformed proper noise/global/slab priors and their proposal ratios. An independence Student-t mixture in `(log sigma2, log u_g, log c2)` has the full multivariate normalization and a nonzero broad component. Frozen data-informed proposal fitting changes the proposal, not the target. Every collapsed model/period move is followed immediately by a matching Gaussian coefficient redraw before coefficient-conditioned updates.

Finite-slab local RHS conditionals reuse the independently validated exact rejection envelope. Global and slab log-scale slices include `-1/2 sum(log v_j+beta_j^2/v_j)`, including the finite-slab variance normalization. A shared slab prior is counted once across groups. The global/local interweaving move preserves products in each active group and restores auxiliary conditionals. Inactive free scales refresh from their normalized priors. The augmented `rhs_ns` prior is not substituted.

An optional frozen reservoir-local proposal addresses poor architecture mixing without changing the model. In log-local coordinates let `h(ell)=2 exp(ell)/(pi(1+exp(2ell)))`, including the log transformation Jacobian. For model m's active reservoir coordinates A_m, its normalized proposal is `q_m(ell_A|log u)=(1-w_m) prod_j h(ell_j) + w_m prod_j [K_m^(-1) sum_k Normal(ell_j; center_mkj+slope_mj*(log u-reference_m),bandwidth_mj)]`, with `0<=w_m<1`. This is a mixture of the entire prior vector and a product of marginal kernel densities. Sampling each coordinate's kernel center independently is required for this particular product density. It is not a single joint-row kernel mixture. All Gaussian kernel constants, mixture constants, and half-Cauchy Jacobians are included. Shifting with a held global scale has unit Jacobian.

For these moves, beta and reservoir half-Cauchy auxiliaries are integrated out. The fixed raw reservoir slot space has prior `prod_j h(ell_j)`. A proposed new architecture/period draws its active reservoir locals from q_m and every inactive reservoir local from h; lag and calendar locals and all global/slab/noise states are held. The full-space local prior/proposal contribution reduces exactly to `C(new)-C(old)`, where `C(m,ell)=sum_(j in A_m) log h(ell_j)-log q_m(ell_A|log u)`. Add this to the usual collapsed Gaussian, architecture/period prior and discrete proposal ratios. No dimension-matching Jacobian is required: the auxiliary space has fixed dimension. Missing inactive prior constants would invalidate this cancellation. The retained prior mixture also bounds `prod h/q_m <= 1/(1-w_m)` independently of dimension, without eliminating any reservoir.

After this partially collapsed move, every reservoir auxiliary is redrawn from `nu_j | lambda_j ~ IG(1,1+lambda_j^(-2))`, even if the proposed architecture was rejected. Only then is beta redrawn before any beta-conditioned local/global/slab update. This restores the marginalized auxiliaries and preserves the required order. The option is disabled by default and consumes no additional random numbers when disabled. Proposal training is computational adaptation on a separate training-only run, frozen before any production chain; a failed convergence screen is not a reason to treat its draws as posterior results, but it does not invalidate using them to design a full-support proposal with exact corrections.

Constant variance has the original IG(a_sigma,b_sigma) prior, defaults (3,1). SV uses `log D_t=log sigma2 + w_t'gamma + omega*u_t`; u_1~N(0,1), u_t|u_(t-1),phi~N(phi*u_(t-1),1-phi^2). `(phi+1)/2~Beta(10,1.5)` and omega~HalfNormal(SD=.5) by default. Initial phi/omega are algorithm controls, excluded from target identity. Seasonal log variance, when enabled, has four centered Fourier harmonics and proper Gaussian coefficients with variance `.25^2/(1+(k/2)^4)`, centered using training timestamps. Period moves change both mean and variance designs. There is no second variance intercept.

The SV update is an exact elliptical-slice Gaussian-prior state update conditional on phi, followed by transformed phi and omega slices and a Gaussian-prior elliptical update for optional gamma. The phi log-coordinate prior is Beta(plogis(z)) times the logistic Jacobian, with the raw phi-to-probability constants cancelling. Its AR(1) transition determinant is retained. The noise update is IG(a_sigma+n/2,b_sigma+sum(residual^2*exp(-w'gamma-omega*u))/2). This conditional would not apply to a residual-defined GARCH variance; that model is not implemented here.

The conditional smoother diagnostic `edf` is computed at the Gaussian update substep as p-tr(K^-1), K=I+V^(1/2)X'D^-1XV^(1/2). It includes the proper intercept and is not a selected-variable count or total adaptive-model degrees of freedom. Later Gibbs substeps change the stored scales, so this substep diagnostic must not be paired with final-row scales as though recomputed at them. Scalar traces and fitted probes remain unthinned; bounded joint coefficient snapshots are explicitly indexed and matched to `fit$draws`.

Group norm, group conditional EDF, final-state Gaussian likelihood and terminal variance summaries are recorded without consuming random numbers. `autodesn_readout_coefficients()` transports each matching draw separately, applying every column center/scale and the response transform. With original-response lag predictors it also subtracts the response-center times the transported lag-slope sum from the intercept. Zero slopes fill dropped constant columns. This is a deterministic change of coordinates for inspection, not an independent RHS prior on transformed coefficients.

The conditional particle filter initializes each selected static draw by replaying the scored training observations from the stationary initial state, not by cloning one terminal draw. Sequential weight normalization and optional ESS-triggered resampling operate only inside that static draw. Between origins, observed states update causally. Static architecture/period/parameter weights remain frozen as required by the experiment. This operational mixture is distinct from full sequential Bayesian reweighting. Within a forecast, architecture/period/static coefficients remain fixed while future volatility and responses are simulated recursively. No future observed response is accepted by the forecast interface.

Recovered compact reservoir objects store terminal layer states in `H_last`, whereas complete builder objects store paths in `H_all`. Both formats now resolve to the same numeric initial layers; missing or malformed states error rather than becoming zeros. Earlier development adapters produced empty initial lists for compact objects. This defect was found before issuing new benchmark forecasts and does not enter the frozen Gaussian design likelihood. The established conditional-bank identifier is preserved for posterior/checkpoint compatibility; corrected forecast states and numeric propagation maps have a separate `forecast_map_id`, computed after the conditional hash. Conditional filter identities additionally hash the actual selected numeric maps, input preprocessing and terminal states. Thus reattaching valid compact forecasting maps does not pretend to change the conditional coefficient posterior, and changing a propagation map invalidates its state-filter cache.

Unused weight-generating distribution closures are not needed after numeric W/Win/Q matrices are frozen. A compact forecasting template may remove them and unused metadata only after verifying identical serial and batched transitions. It must retain exact activations, input preprocessing, leak coefficients, projection matrices and terminal states. Its last observed raw features must reproduce the frozen training design row for every architecture. The inference-only matrix cache remains explicitly forbidden for forecast/update calls until real numeric forecasting templates are attached.

The native GP addition changes only the proposal. A period leaf address is selected uniformly, then proposed in its native Gaussian coordinate from a broad Normal(0,1)/training-periodogram-centered normal mixture. Its exact log acceptance ratio is native log-joint difference plus reverse-minus-forward mixture density. Address counts are unchanged, so their selection terms cancel. Native `Gen.update` weights are checked against direct native score differences; covariance grammar, prior multiplicity, jitter and transforms remain native. Canonical syntax merging is not performed in inference.

Bounded joint checkpoints can optionally retain complete latent smoothing paths and generate conditional fixed-training-design replicates. Current limitations include no unconditional response-adaptive recursive PPC calibration, no completed full-size posterior qualification, and no completed independent confirmatory comparison. Numerical tests, successful installation and short feasibility pilots do not remove these limitations.

SV conditional targets are factored into shared private helpers and checked against independently built dense stationary AR(1) covariances, raw/transformed numerical normalizers, and invariant slice moments. Exact zero residuals contribute zero quadratic even if an extreme log variance would overflow a naive exponential; nonfinite proposed log variances have zero density. Forecast batching groups only deterministic reservoir work. Terminal filtering draws are sampled in original outer-draw order, and full path/draw assignments are retained.
